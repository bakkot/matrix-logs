<Ashley Claymore>
From my July slides, the benefit of caching unsurprisingly increases with the number of keys.
Microbenchmark of a perfect sort cache (cache always hit, ~no lookup overhead) based on the shape showed:
3 properties: 0.53 µs → 0.46 µs (12% faster)
16 properties: 0.91 µs → 0.65 µs per composite (29% faster)
250 properties: 10.34 µs → 4.72 µs per composite (54% faster)

<Ashley Claymore>
